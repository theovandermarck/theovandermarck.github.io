FRC 9038 2026 Competition Robot: Claude

Design Lead, Team Co-Captain · CAD, Project Management, CAM, 3D Printing · 6061 T6 Aluminum Extrusion & Plates/Polycarbonate/TPU/CF-Nylon · Jan 2026 - Apr 2026

Turreted ball shooting robot with 40 ball capacity inside a 28" x 27" x 26" starting footprint. Utilized a turret, computer vision, and a variable angle shooter to easily score up to 9 6" diameter balls/second from anywhere in the allowable area. Won 3 playoff matches across two competitive events as the main scoring robot and won Excellence in Engineering at the East Bay District Event, by far the best performance in our team's history.

I personally led the full design process, ideating and creating the top-down layout, the 1.25" tall turret assembly, the drivebase, and the spindexer. I also led other team members through the design and fabrication of the shooter, climber, kicker indexer, and intake.

(Please note that this robot was named after the beloved albino alligator at the California Academy of Sciences, not the generative AI model.)

Solo Build · CAD, High-Speed Power Transmission, 3D Printing, Electrical Work, Soldering · Carbon Fiber/Aluminum/UHMW/TPU/AR500 Steel · Jun 2025 - Nov 2025

Mechanical Clock

Solo Build · CAD, Geometry Iteration, 3D Printing · PETG · Jun 2026 - Aug 2026

Fully mechanical clock (regulated by a swinging pendulum and powered by a hanging weight) tuned to tick at the rate of true time. As the underlying principle of a mechanical clock is that the hanging weight imparts energy back into the pallet forks equal to that lost over time, this project required more than 20 rounds of iteration on geometries for escapement wheels, pallet forks, pendulums, and gears. Due to unavoidable limits in material strength and available space the clock only runs for about 90 minutes, but runs relatively accurately and without ever stopping for that duration. For this project I worked on more thoroughly planning out my ideas before starting CAD and during the CAD process, which was very helpful for saving time and organizing my process.

FRC 9038 2025 Competition Robot: Alcatraz

Design Co-Lead, Team Co-Captain · CAD, Project Management, Power Transmission Design, 3D Printing · 6061 T6 Aluminum Extrusion & Plates/Polycarbonate/PETG-CF/TPU · Jan 2025 - May 2025

Reliable pick-and-place robot designed for the 2025 FRC game. Utilized a multi-purpose end effector, a single stage elevator, and a hot-swappable (fully replacable within 5 minutes with 2 different mechanisms depending on the match) slapdown intake. Had almost zero mechanical issues at competition, and achieved the team's best ever (at the time) competition season.

I worked with two other design leads to design the robot, personally pushing the team towards our final overall archetype and leading the 5-person team designing the robust, easily repairable, and hot-swappable slapdown ground intake. I also worked as a co-captain to build knowledge-transfer systems (we'd faced issues as a non-mentored team when all of our seniors graduated) and ensure team longevity, work that alongside efforts from the other two co-captains won our team the Rising All Star award for our successes in full-student leadership.
